Why Dentra exists

Small clinics deserve enterprise-grade ops — without enterprise pricing

Most practices stitch together spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, and folder trees. Cloud PMS tools add monthly fees, internet dependency, and features you never asked for. Dentra is the opposite: a serious, feature-complete platform that runs on your hardware, stays under your control, and costs a fraction of recurring SaaS.

Platform & deployment

Built like a product. Shipped like an appliance.

Node.js and SQLite under the hood, a polished SPA on top, and Windows-first tooling so any clinic can install, update, and run it — no IT department required.

🖥️

Windows-first deployment

Portable Node, one-click launchers, optional Windows service, and a proper Inno Setup installer — ready for the clinic PC on day one.

🔄

In-app updates

Staff check and apply new releases from Settings. No manual file swaps, no developer visits.

👤

Role-based access

Admin, dentist, and receptionist roles with permissions tuned to how each person actually works.

💾

Backup & restore

Full database backup, Excel import for patient lists, and export when you need a portable snapshot.
